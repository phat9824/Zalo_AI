## Competition Timeline

### I. Public Test Round (October 26 – November 16)

- Participating teams build their own solutions, run them on the organizers’ public test set, and submit result files (JSONL) via the **Submission** section on the Zalo AI Challenge portal.
- Teams are allowed up to **5 submissions per day**, and the quota will be reset at the end of each day.
- Scores and rankings will be displayed on the public leaderboard.

#### Final Solution Submission — November 16

On November 16, participating teams must submit their complete solutions, including:

- Training code.
- Inference code.
- Data.

These must be submitted in the form of a Docker package to the organizers via the **Solution Submission** form on the Zalo AI Challenge portal.

Since the Docker file is usually quite large, teams should provide:

- A checksum.
- A public Google Drive link so that the organizer can download it.

**Submission Rules:**

- Each team is allowed up to **2 submissions**.
- The organizer will only evaluate the last submission.
- **Submission deadline:** 23:59 (GMT+7).

### II. Private Test Round (November 20 – November 23)

- At **9:00 AM on November 20**, the organizer will release the private test set.
- Participating teams will run their submitted solutions on this set and submit the result files (JSONL) via the **Submission** section on the Zalo AI Challenge portal.
- **Submission deadline:** 23:59 (GMT+7) on November 23.
- The private leaderboard will be displayed in chronological order (based on submission time).

### Evaluation and Results

The organizer will evaluate the submitted solutions to:

- Verify results.
- Review source code.
- Detect any cheating.

The **top 2 teams** will be announced on **December 2**.
